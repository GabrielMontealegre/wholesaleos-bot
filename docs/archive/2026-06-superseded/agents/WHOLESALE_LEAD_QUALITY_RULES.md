# Wholesale Lead Quality Rules

## Good Candidate

A candidate can enter the working acquisition flow when it has:

- usable property address or property identity
- property-specific public source URL or exact official/source reference
- selected market match
- at least one concrete requested criterion
- no source/address conflict
- clear next action

## Research Ready

Research Ready means the card can go to AI Deal Analyzer for source and comp research. It does not mean valuation is unlocked.

Required:

- property-specific source or exact source proof
- usable address or identity
- in-market or likely in-market
- non-generic source
- no access/login/CAPTCHA blocker

## Call Today

Call Today requires Research Ready plus a real contact path or source/listing route. Do not invent contact information.

## Blocked Categories

- Needs Source Proof: generic/search/category/homepage source, missing exact property proof, or source evidence too broad.
- Needs Address Repair: missing, incomplete, conflicting, junk, or non-property-specific address.
- Support Signal Only: useful distress signal, but not enough alone.
- Junk / Archive: wrong market, blocked source, duplicate, generic noise, or no usable property identity.

## Default Batch Rules

- Default wholesale-safe batch excludes auction, REO, bank-owned, generic pages, no-address rows, wrong-market rows, duplicates, Bad Lead, and comp-only/low-value rows.
- Auction/REO can be reviewed only when explicitly included.
- Manual Review Queue must receive blocked promising source rows with exact blocker reasons.
