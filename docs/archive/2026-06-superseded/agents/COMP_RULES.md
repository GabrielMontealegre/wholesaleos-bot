# Comp Rules

## Verified Sold Comp

A verified sold comp must be a different property from the subject and include:

- address
- sold or closed status
- sold price
- sold date or timing
- source URL

Subject sale evidence does not count as a comp.

## Buckets

- Verified Sold Comps: all required evidence present and different from subject.
- Candidate Sold Comps: likely sold comp but missing one or more required fields.
- Market Support: active, pending, listing, nearby value indicator, tax assessment, source estimate, or general market context.
- Not Usable / Excluded: generic results, subject property as its own comp, duplicates, wrong market, missing source, or unclear status.

## Valuation Gates

- 0 verified different comps: ARV locked.
- 1-2 verified different comps: ARV locked.
- 3+ verified different comps: preliminary evidence-backed ARV may be allowed.
- Candidate comps do not unlock ARV.
- Market Support does not unlock ARV.
- Tax assessments, source estimates, nearby values, auction bids, and list prices do not unlock ARV.
- MAO remains locked unless ARV exists and repair evidence or manual repair estimate exists.

## Forbidden

Do not fake comps, ARV, MAO, sold price, sold date, DOM, tax amount, repair amount, or listing history.
