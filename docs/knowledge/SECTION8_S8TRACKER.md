# Section 8: what S8Tracker / Academia Sección 8 teaches, and how we use it

Source: the public pages of s8tracker.com (home, /software, /mentoria, /resultados, /mapa), read 2026-10-07. The summary below is in our own words. The site calls itself educational, not a broker or lender, and its figures are its own projections.

## 1. What it is

- **Academia Sección 8 LLC** runs a Spanish-language mentorship (mentor: Ali Abdul Hadi, teaching since 2022, "600+ students").
- Their software, **S8Tracker**, comes only with the mentorship. It is not sold separately and the price isn't published.
- **The business is buy-and-hold, not wholesaling.** Students buy houses, rent them to Section 8 voucher tenants, and keep them.
- **Who it's for:** citizens or foreigners, with or without US credit, buying 100% remotely. Each student needs about **$20k** cash per house (20–25% down).

## 2. How their software finds deals (the method)

1. **Map of 3,220 counties.** Each county is colored by the program rent (HUD FMR / payment standard) per bedroom count versus home prices.
2. **Price forecast (12 months)** from 5 signals: appreciation, days on market, mortgage rates, inventory, and price cuts. The idea is to buy before prices rise.
3. **Property finder:** about 2M listings (MLS/Zillow/Redfin), 40+ filters (price, net cash flow, beds, area income, owner financing). It flags listings that look like **owner financing** and drops anything under **$60k**.
4. **Cash flow per house**, in three scenarios (down payment 20–25%, rate, term):
   - rent = Section 8 rent for that county/ZIP and bedroom count;
   - minus management 10%, maintenance + vacancy 10%, taxes, insurance, and the mortgage;
   - shows ROI, cap rate and cash-on-cash.
5. **Financing:** **DSCR loans** (qualify on the house's rent, not salary; works for foreigners) or **owner financing** (sometimes 5–10% down).
6. **Execution:** offer templates, a network of ~240 vetted contacts (property managers, attorneys, CPAs; 16 specialties, 42 markets), and remote closing.

**Their typical target:** houses at **$80k–$150k**, **$400–$700/month net** per house, tenants who stay **5–7 years**, and the voucher paying most or all of the rent.

**Markets they show most:**
- Cleveland OH: high rent-to-price, about 15,000+ vouchers;
- Jackson MS: very high ratio, ~$88k typical price;
- Birmingham AL: ~$1,583 for a 3-bed;
- Indianapolis IN: example $1,400 FMR, +$512/month.

## 3. What this means for WholesaleOS (our use)

**A. Section 8 investors are BUYERS for our wholesale deals.** Hundreds of Spanish-speaking, often foreign, investors want **$80k–$150k houses** that pass inspection in Cleveland, Jackson, Birmingham, Indianapolis and similar markets (Detroit too). The problem they solve is finding the house, and that is what a wholesaler sells. Add them as a buyer segment:
- Facebook groups and pages in Spanish ("inversionistas sección 8", "casas sección 8", "DSCR extranjeros");
- an outreach message in Spanish;
- buy box = market + beds + max price + "inspection-ready or light rehab".

**B. Add a "Section 8 lens" to every deal card (Deal Check #275).** Next to the flip math (75% ARV minus repairs), show:
- Section 8 rent: HUD Small Area FMR for that ZIP and bedroom count (#275 already plans the HUD SAFMR rent);
- estimated net cash flow at 20% down: rent − 10% management − 10% maintenance/vacancy − taxes − insurance − mortgage (label the rate assumption and date);
- verdict "Works for a Section 8 buyer" when net ≥ about $300/month and the price is in the buyer's range.
This turns a deal that's too thin for a flipper into one a rental buyer will take.

**C. Inspection matters.** Voucher tenants need a passing HQS/NSPIRE inspection. Deals needing heavy rehab fit a flipper; "light rehab, inspection-ready" fits a Section 8 buyer.

**D. For Gabriel personally (later, not advice).** The same model (DSCR, LLC, remote close) is open to foreigners. If he ever wants to hold rentals, talk to a CPA first (FIRPTA/taxes, D-045) and a lender.

## 4. Cautions

- The returns quoted are marketing projections. Check rent with the **local housing authority's payment standard**, not only FMR, and check real taxes and insurance.
- Cheap markets ($60k–$100k) carry more vacancy, repair and crime risk. Their own software drops listings under $60k.
- We only use public information. We don't scrape their paid app or copy their course.
